### SRC-11 spike evidence (2026-09-14, spike-runner agent) — ADOPT (scoped): store-with-flag, lead-window similarity

Hypothesis: cosine(question, FULL plain-text extract) separates the
known doppelganger pages from correct pages well enough for a
threshold/relative rule at evidence-storage time — where SRC-10 proved
title+snippet signals fail. 18 live calls (4 searches + 14 extracts),
sequential, zero 429s; script exit 0.

**Scores (whole-extract cosine, question-prefixed query):** correct
pages 0.74–0.85 (WWW 0.837, History-WWW 0.827, TimBL 0.7375, Aurora
0.815, Peace of Westphalia 0.853, Berlin Wall 0.794, Fall of BW 0.780);
dopps 0.53–0.79 (MikeBL 0.530, album 0.637, singer 0.674, concert 0.653;
adjacent Q04 family 0.746–0.792).

**Decision rules:** absolute threshold **t = 0.70 (whole) drops exactly
the 4 clearly-off-topic dopps with 0/7 correct pages dropped** (any t in
(0.674, 0.7375) works; margin +0.063, thin at n=14). Relative rule:
whole α=0.85 → 4/7 dopps, 0 false positives (α=0.9 drops TimBL — do not
exceed 0.85 on whole scores). Rank rule: correct page rank-1 in 4/4
questions. Adjacent Q04 family survives every FP-free rule — this check
does not fix Q04's F3 problem, it removes clearly-off-topic pages.

**Chunking insight (key design fact):** whole > chunkMean > chunkMax,
and chunking is 7× slower (880 ms vs 6407 ms per extract). fastembed
silently truncates to ~512 tokens → "whole-extract" similarity is
effectively LEAD-WINDOW similarity — the intro states what the article
is about, which is precisely why it separates. Production must document
this honestly (not claim full-document similarity).

**Adopted design (store-with-flag):** in the evidence-storage path
(harness, after fetch/parse, before passage chunking) compute
`cos(embedQuery(question), embedPassages(extract))` once per source
(whole, unchunked — accepted-lead semantics, ~0.9 s CPU per source, no
extra network). `score < floor` (config default 0.70, tunable) → mark
evidence `relevance: low`: keep the receipts, exclude flagged sources
from the answer/synthesis pool and UI prominence. Advisory only: if
embedding is unavailable, store unflagged, never block the pipeline.
The 14 measured pairs freeze as an offline fixture eval so CI sensors
judge threshold changes.

fallback (implemented) vs floor applied only to corpus-fallback evidence.
